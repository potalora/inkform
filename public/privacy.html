Privacy and storage

Inkform Beta stores your projects and imported artwork in this browser on this device, using IndexedDB. There are no Inkform accounts or cloud project sync. The application does not upload your projects or artwork.

Browser storage is not a backup. Clearing site data, using a private browsing session, browser storage eviction, or losing this device can remove your work. Export a project backup and keep it somewhere separate. Import that backup to move your work to another browser or device.

Inkform requests persistent browser storage when available. Your browser may refuse or later clear it. Saving can also fail when storage is full; keep the editor open and export your work if a save fails.

The hosting provider, Cloudflare, receives normal page and asset requests, including network information such as your IP address. Inkform introduces no analytics, advertising trackers, or application cookies. The application's runtime models, fonts and scripts are hosted with the site.

This is a limited beta. Physical iPhone and Android acceptance remains pending. Device storage behavior and rendering support may vary.
