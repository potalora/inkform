The flat tattoo treatment bed, oak cabinet, room architecture, planter, olive tree,
supply bottles, and generated oak grain were authored specifically for
this project. These original generated assets are dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The photographed wall and floor PBR textures in textures/ are separate
Poly Haven CC0 assets. Their original authors, download URLs, physical sizes,
source checksums and local SHA256 hashes are recorded in textures/provenance.json
and textures/ATTRIBUTION.md. Poly Haven's asset license is published at:
https://polyhaven.com/license

The Three.js GLTF loader is separately MIT licensed; see three.LICENSE.txt.
The existing studio_small_09_1k.hdr retains its own existing Poly Haven
provenance at ../provenance.json.
